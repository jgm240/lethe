Search suggestions
Show suggestions while you type. With DuckDuckGo, what you type is sent to DuckDuckGo to get suggestions.
Tell duckduckgo.com I'm using a DuckDuckGo browser
Stops DuckDuckGo from showing its “get our browser” promotions at the source, the same way its own browser does. This sends duckduckgo.com a user agent that matches DuckDuckGo's browser, so DuckDuckGo may count you as one of its browser users. Only used on duckduckgo.com.
Hide DuckDuckGo browser promotions
Replaces the “Download our browser” banners and “Switch to DuckDuckGo” prompts on DuckDuckGo pages with a short note, and hides the Downloads menu section.

Privacy

Block trackers
Blocks third-party tracking requests (advertising, analytics, fingerprinting scripts).
Upgrade to HTTPS
Opens encrypted versions of sites whenever possible, and falls back if a site doesn't support it.
Global Privacy Control
Tells websites not to sell or share your data.
Block third-party cookies
Stops embedded content from setting cookies that follow you between sites.
Link tracking protection
Removes tracking parameters such as utm_* and fbclid from the links you open.
Cookie pop-up manager
Automatically picks “reject” or “necessary only” on cookie banners when it can, and never accepts.
Save browsing history
Stored on this device only.

Fire Button

Burn data when InstaOlvidar closes

Burn everything now

Content & Location

Page content

No CSS
Loads pages without any site styling, so you see plain, readable HTML. Blocks stylesheets, inline styles and style attributes.
No images
Doesn't load images, which saves data and removes image-based tracking pixels.
No JavaScript
Blocks all scripts on websites. Many sites (logins, video, maps, search suggestions) won't work. Applies to every site; the InstaOlvidar Player and these settings pages are not affected.
Changes apply the next time a page loads. The ⋯ menu has quick toggles under “Page Content”.

Location

Spoof location
Websites that ask where you are get the place below instead of your real location, and your time zone matches it. This does not hide your IP address, so sites that locate you by IP will still see your real region. Use a VPN for that.
Place
Choose on a map
Click anywhere on the map, or drag the pin, to set your fake location. The time zone follows automatically. While the map is open it loads tiles from OpenStreetMap, which can see your IP address and the area you view. Nothing is contacted until you open it.
Custom place
Latitude (−90 to 90), longitude (−180 to 180) and an IANA time zone such as Europe/Vienna.

User agent

Change user agent
Tells websites you're using a different browser or device. Sites may serve a different layout (phone layouts for mobile choices) and some may break. This changes what sites are told, not other signals such as screen size or fonts. Applies to the next page load.
Custom user agent
Plain text only. Leave it empty to use the default.
