Search suggestions
Show suggestions while you type. With DuckDuckGo, what you type is sent to DuckDuckGo to get suggestions.
Tell duckduckgo.com I'm using a DuckDuckGo browser
Stops DuckDuckGo from showing its “get our browser” promotions at the source, the same way its own browser does. This sends duckduckgo.com a user agent that matches DuckDuckGo's browser, so DuckDuckGo may count you as one of its browser users. Only used on duckduckgo.com.
Hide DuckDuckGo browser promotions
Replaces the “Download our browser” banners and “Switch to DuckDuckGo” prompts on DuckDuckGo pages with a short note, and hides the Downloads menu section.

Eject to Lethe

Eject when Lethe closes
Automatically erases history, cookies and site data on quit.
Eject now
Closes all tabs and erases data (fireproof sites keep their cookies). The ⋯ button next to the address bar has quicker options, such as forgetting only the last hour.

Fireproof sites

These sites keep their cookies (so you stay signed in) when you use the Eject to Lethe. Add one from the shield menu.

Sites with protections off

Eject & Forget

Canal to Lethe
Everything flows away as you go. Nothing is recorded (no history, closed tabs or downloads list). A site’s cookies, storage and cache are erased the moment no tab shows it, leftovers are swept every 30 seconds, and the whole profile is wiped when Lethe opens and closes. Fireproof sites are the only exception. Bookmarks and settings stay.
Ask before ejecting
Show a confirmation before anything is erased.
River animation
Scrolls are thrown into the river of forgetting when you eject everything.
Eject or forget now
“Eject to Lethe” closes every tab and erases everything. The other two erase the sites you visited in that window (cookies, site data and history), skipping fireproof sites.

Auto-forget

Forget sites I haven't visited for…
Sites that have sat untouched this long are forgotten automatically (never one that is open or fireproof). Lethe remembers only which sites you visited, never pages, to be able to do this.
Forget when the tab closes
These sites lose their cookies and data as soon as no tab is showing them. You can also switch this on from the shield menu.

Content & Location

Page content

No CSS
Loads pages without any site styling, so you see plain, readable HTML. Blocks stylesheets, inline styles and style attributes.
No images
Doesn't load images, which saves data and removes image-based tracking pixels.
No JavaScript
Blocks all scripts on websites. Many sites (logins, video, maps, search suggestions) won't work. Applies to every site; the Lethe Player and these settings pages are not affected.
Changes apply the next time a page loads. The ⋯ menu has quick toggles under “Page Content”.

Location

Spoof location
Websites that ask where you are get the place below instead of your real location, and your time zone matches it. This does not hide your IP address, so sites that locate you by IP will still see your real region. Use a VPN for that.
Place
Choose on a map
Click anywhere on the map, or drag the pin, to set your fake location. The time zone follows automatically. While the map is open it loads tiles from OpenStreetMap, which can see your IP address and the area you view. Nothing is contacted until you open it.
Custom place
Latitude (−90 to 90), longitude (−180 to 180) and an IANA time zone such as Europe/Vienna.

User agent

Change user agent
Tells websites you're using a different browser or device. Sites may serve a different layout (phone layouts for mobile choices) and some may break. This changes what sites are told, not other signals such as screen size or fonts. Applies to the next page load.
Custom user agent
Plain text only. Leave it empty to use the default.
